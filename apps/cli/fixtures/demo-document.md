# Demo Document for Refusal Block Testing

This document contains deliberately truncated Quran and Hadith quotes to test the refusal block rendering (MS2-4).

## Section 1: Truncated Quran Quote

The Quran says: "إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ" — this is an incomplete quote from the famous hadith about intentions, but presented as a Quran quote. It has fewer than 15 Arabic characters after normalization.

## Section 2: Another Short Quote

Reference: "وَالْعَصْرِ" — just the opening oath of Surah Al-Asr, only 8 Arabic characters.

## Section 3: Valid Length Quote (should verify)

The complete hadith: "إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَاتِ وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى" — this should verify correctly as it's the full text from Bukhari.

## Section 4: Truncated Hadith Quote

Bukhari reports: "مَنْ سَرَّهُ أَنْ يُزَحْزَحَ" — truncated version of a longer hadith, only 14 Arabic characters.

## Section 5: Very Short Fragment

Just two words: "الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ" — only 11 Arabic characters including the definite article.
